# Hackathon requirements (condensed; read before README, video or Devpost work)

Source: the University Blockchain Conference (UBC 2026) organizers' guide, pasted by the team on 2026-11-17. This is a condensed summary, NOT a verbatim copy. Re-check the Devpost page (ubc-hackathon-2026.devpost.com) for anything that matters.

## Dates (CST unless noted)
- Hackathon start Thu Nov 12, 6:00 PM. Submission deadline Fri Nov 20, 12:00 PM CST (1:00 PM ET). Ignore Devpost's own Friday-noon start.
- Finalists (up to 13) are notified by Fri Nov 20, 11:59 PM CST. Judging, final pitches and winners are Sat Nov 21 at the closing ceremony.
- Each finalist pitches 5 minutes plus 2 minutes Q&A, in person. Teams must be present in person for the closing ceremony to qualify for ANY prize.

## Our entries
- Main track (exactly one): Agentic Payments and AI. Chain award (one of Solana or Arc, never both): Best use of Solana. Bounties are optional: we do NOT qualify for QuickNode Streams (we use a QuickNode RPC only, not Streams), Gemini, ElevenLabs or Grok Bot; do not apply to them.
- Prizes stack (Track Winner $2,000 + Best use of Solana $1,500 + Official Winner), except Solana and Arc never both.

## Track requirements: Agentic Payments and AI
- Agents execute onchain transactions or read the chain (devnet fine).
- Document the agent's decision loop and the safety limits (budgets, allowlists, human approval for large transactions are the examples given). Recommended tools include x402.

## Best use of Solana
- Core onchain functionality on Solana (devnet fine); using existing programs counts.
- README must include the program ID(s) the project deploys or uses and explain how it uses Solana. The guide does not name a specific program ID.

## What to submit (all required for the participation prize)
- Project name, short description, all team members; main track, chain award, bounties.
- Written answers: what did you build, what problem does it solve, how does it work, what makes it unique, what would you build next.
- Technologies and sponsor tools used.
- Link to the public GitHub repo with source and setup instructions, built during the hackathon.
- A working demo or deployed application.
- 2-3 minute demo video (Google Drive or YouTube link, accessible to judges; our own cap is 3:00).
- README with technical documentation and architecture overview.
- Track-specific items above.

## Judging criteria
Technical execution; product and UX; innovation (originality); impact and potential; demo quality and clarity.

## Other rules
- Pre-existing projects not allowed; AI tools are allowed. Teams of 1-4. Prizes are at the judges' discretion; participation prize needs a complete, running submission.
